Reporting a bug

The more of this you include, the faster it gets fixed:

  • What you were doing when it happened
  • What you expected instead
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • A screenshot, if there was something to see

Your data

Everything you log stays on your iPhone. Arete has no account and makes no network calls, so we never see your data. To keep a copy or move it to a new phone, use More → Other Settings → Export & Import in the app. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
